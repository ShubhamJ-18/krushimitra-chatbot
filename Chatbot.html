🗺️ Crop Planning & Seasonal Rotation Assistant
Scientific crop rotations recommended by Maharashtra Department of Agriculture & MPKV Rahuri to break pest cycles and conserve soil.

🌱 Rotation 1: Soybean – Chickpea (हरभरा)

Ideal Zone: Marathwada & Vidarbha (Medium to deep black soil).

Why it works: Soybean matures in 90-100 days leaving residual nitrogen and moisture. Minimum-till Chickpea requires only 2-3 irrigations in winter.

Trade-offs: Moderate water requirement, excellent soil fertility maintenance.

🌾 Rotation 2: Cotton + Pigeonpea (तूर) Intercropping

Ideal Zone: Khandesh & Vidarbha.

Why it works: 6:1 or 8:1 row ratio. Deep tap root of Pigeonpea breaks soil hardpan and acts as biological buffer against pests.

Trade-offs: Long duration, risk reduction against erratic monsoon.

💧 Rotation 3: Bajra – Mustard / Chickpea

Ideal Zone: Drought-prone zones (Ahmednagar, Solapur, Beed).

Why it works: Requires minimal water, thrives in shallow soils (<30 cm), low investment cost.

Trade-offs: Lower gross revenue but lowest climate risk.
